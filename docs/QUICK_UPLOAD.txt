GOMOVIE - QUICK UPLOAD GUIDE
===========================

Upload Studio: /upload-studio

For a new video:
1. Enter the video title.
2. Select an MP4, MOV, MKV, or WebM file.
3. Choose a viewing option and confirm you have permission to use the video.
4. Click Upload Video once.

Viewing options
---------------
FREE / PUBLIC
The original file is stored privately. When this installation has a working
media worker, FFmpeg, ClamAV, and public HLS delivery enabled, Gomovie
automatically checks the uploaded file and publishes an approved FREE title.

The uploader must declare that they own the video or possess WORLDWIDE online
publishing permission. This is a recorded uploader declaration, NOT an
independently verified distribution license. Remove unauthorized content
promptly when notified, and honor rights revocation.

PRIVATE
Store the video privately without starting automatic public publication.

PAID VIDEO / DRAFT
Save the movie as a premium draft. No paid checkout, collection, commission,
rental, subscription or downloadable resale license is activated. Do not
advertise paid delivery until a payment provider, access controls, refunds,
tax/receipt responsibilities, and transaction reconciliation are verified.

Editing existing movies or TV episodes
--------------------------------------
The old catalog editor is still available from the Advanced editor link. It is
not part of the normal upload process. Existing uploads and other movie records
are not changed by Quick Upload.

Hosting
-------
PHP file size limits and web server body size limits can restrict uploads.
The current single-request intake ceiling is 128 MB; the displayed upload
limit is the lower of PHP settings and that ceiling. Larger, resumable
uploads need a separate implementation. A dedicated background media worker
and FFmpeg/ClamAV are required for automatic public processing.

Marketplace delivery
--------------------
Sell the SCRIPT separately from the VIDEO CONTENT. Do not include unlicensed
movies, film posters, actor images or copyrighted sample clips in the buyer ZIP.
Use original demo video and artwork or appropriately licensed alternatives.
Real online sales of video content require additional payment and legal work.

No manual media approval step is required for a newly uploaded original
free video with a recorded admin permission declaration, but all server-side
validation, media integrity, private storage, malware checks, and the ability
to revoke public playback remain in place.
